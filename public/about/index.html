about
our story started in italy, rome Founded in 2025 as a family-driven company, RomArte Interiors was born from a passion for blending Italian elegance with Egyptian heritage. What started as a vision is now shaping into a studio dedicated to creating timeless, sophisticated, and functional spaces.

With a deep appreciation for architectural harmony and craftsmanship, we aspire to grow, evolving into a leading name in bespoke interior design—where every project is a masterpiece tailored to our clients' unique stories.
